Profile.

The author, briefly

For the past five years I've designed, built and shipped software across the stack — clean APIs, snappy interfaces, and infrastructure that doesn't fall over at 3 a.m. I write code the way good books are typeset: with rhythm, restraint, and respect for whoever reads it next.

I care about performance budgets, readable diffs and interfaces that feel alive. Currently taking on ambitious projects and full-time roles.
